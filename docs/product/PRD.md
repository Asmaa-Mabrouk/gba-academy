# PRD: Global Business Academy website

The full PRD (users, scope, requirements tables, release plan, risks) was written and shared as a Claude document. Paste its final approved text here to keep the repository self-contained.

## Summary of scope
- Learners register and log in; explore courses by audience (kids 6-12, youth 13-24, experts), level, topic and format; buy recorded, live or blended offerings; learn; get certificates; rate courses and mentors.
- Kids' courses are bought by a parent or guardian account.
- Mentors have public profiles with ratings and comments.
- Payment (mada, Apple Pay, cards), ZATCA e-invoicing and video are delivered by a developer behind interfaces.
- Stack: PostgreSQL, Java (Spring Boot), TypeScript (Next.js).
